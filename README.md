**Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard | Power BI**

**Project Overview**

In this project, I analyzed a telecom customer churn dataset using Power BI to understand the factors contributing to customer churn. The dashboard highlights customer demographics, payment behavior, contract details, internet services, and support services to identify patterns and provide business recommendations for improving customer retention.

**Objective**

The main objective of this project was to identify the key factors influencing customer churn and present meaningful insights through an interactive dashboard that can support business decision-making.

**Dataset**

- Telecom Customer Churn Dataset
- Total Customers: 7,043
- Churned Customers: 1,869
- Active Customers: 5,174

**Tools & Technologies**

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Visualization

**Business Findings**

- Overall customer churn rate is **26.54%.**
- Customers with **0–12 months** tenure are more likely to churn.
- **Month-to-month contract** customers have the highest churn rate.
- Customers using **Fiber Optic internet** show **higher churn** than other internet service users.
- **Electronic Check** is the most common payment method among churned customers.
- Customers **without technical support or online security** are more likely to leave.
- **Senior citizens** have a higher churn rate compared to non-senior customers.

**Recommendations**

- Improve customer engagement during the first year.
- Encourage customers to switch to long-term contracts.
- Promote automatic payment methods.
- Offer bundled plans that include technical support and online security.
- Focus retention campaigns on high-risk customer groups.
- Review pricing strategies for customers with higher monthly charges.
